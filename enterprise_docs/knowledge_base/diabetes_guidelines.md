# Underwriting Manual: Diabetes Mellitus (Type 2)

**Document ID**: UW-MAN-001
**Applies To**: All Health Retail Products (Standard, Gold, Platinum)

## 1. Definition and Risk
Diabetes Mellitus is a chronic condition characterized by elevated blood sugar. For underwriting purposes, we strictly differentiate between controlled and uncontrolled diabetes due to the severe long-term risk of cardiovascular, renal, and ophthalmic complications.

## 2. Mandatory Required Documents
Any disclosure of Diabetes requires the following mandatory documents before an underwriting decision can be made:
- Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS)
- Post Prandial Blood Sugar (PPBS)
- HbA1c (must be within the last 30 days)

*If applicant age > 50, additionally request:*
- ECG
- Serum Creatinine
- Routine Urine Examination (RUE)

## 3. Decision Matrix (Based on HbA1c)
- **HbA1c < 6.5%**: Controlled. Accept at Standard Rates.
- **HbA1c 6.5% - 7.0%**: Mildly Uncontrolled. Accept with 10% Premium Loading on base rate.
- **HbA1c 7.1% - 8.0%**: Uncontrolled. 
  - *Standard/Gold Plan*: Decline.
  - *Platinum Plan*: Accept with 20% Premium Loading.
- **HbA1c > 8.0%**: Severely Uncontrolled. Decline across all retail plans.

## 4. Co-Morbidities (Compound Risk)
If the applicant discloses Diabetes AND Hypertension (BP > 140/90), apply a compound loading of 25% if HbA1c is < 7.0%. If HbA1c > 7.0% with Hypertension, Auto-Decline.
