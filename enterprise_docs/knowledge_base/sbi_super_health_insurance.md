# Underwriting Manual: SBI General Super Health Insurance

**Document ID**: UW-PROD-SBI-001
**Product Type**: Comprehensive Health Insurance (Individual & Family Floater)
**UIN**: SBIHLIP23050V012223

## 1. Product Eligibility & Entry Age
- **Individual Basis**: Self, legally married spouse, children, parents, siblings, grandparents, grandchildren, in-laws, and other relations with insurable interest.
- **Family Floater Basis**: Self, legally married spouse, dependent children (natural/adopted), Parents, and/or Parents-in-law. (Max 4 adults and any number of children).
- **Entry Age Limits**: 
  - Adults: Minimum 18 Years. No maximum restriction on entry age.
  - Dependent Children: 91 days to 30 years.
- **Sum Insured Options**: ₹3 Lacs to ₹2 Crores.

## 2. Core Coverage (Inpatient & Outpatient)
- **Inpatient Hospitalisation**: Covers room rent, boarding, ICU/ICCU, nursing, surgeon/specialist fees, anesthesia, blood, oxygen, OT charges, and diagnostic procedures.
- **Shared Accommodation Benefit**: Daily cash of ₹500/₹1000 (up to max ₹15000) if hospitalisation exceeds 48 hours in shared accommodation at a Network Provider.
- **Pre & Post Hospitalisation**: 
  - Pre-hospitalisation: 60 Days
  - Post-hospitalisation: Up to 180 Days
- **Day Care & Domiciliary**: Covered up to Base Sum Insured.
- **Home Health Care**: Covered if prescribed in writing by treating Medical Practitioner.
- **Ambulance**: Road Ambulance covered up to Base Sum Insured. Air Ambulance (Domestic) covered up to ₹10 Lacs for Emergency Care.
- **Organ Donor Expenses**: Covered up to Base Sum Insured for harvesting the donated organ.

## 3. Specialized & Advanced Treatments
- **Bariatric Surgery Cover**: Covered if medically necessary and advised by a Medical Practitioner.
- **Modern Treatments/Advanced Procedures**: Covered as Inpatient or Day Care.
- **AYUSH Treatment**: Covered for Ayurveda, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy inpatient care.
- **Maternity & New Born Baby**: Covered up to ₹2,00,000 (includes pre/post-natal check-ups).
- **Annual Health Check-up**: Preventive check-up up to ₹10,000 per year (from 1st renewal).
- **Recovery Benefit**: Lump sum up to ₹10,000 for hospitalisations exceeding 5 consecutive days (over and above Base Sum Insured).

## 4. Key Differentiators & Multipliers
- **Health Multiplier Benefit (HM)**: For 37 Listed Serious Illnesses, the Base Sum Insured is multiplied up to 3 times (3x) at no extra cost.
- **ReInsure Benefit (RI)**: Unlimited reinstatement of Sum Insured (up to 200% refill) for both related and unrelated illnesses. Payable from the very first claim.
- **Enhanced Cumulative Bonus (ECB)**: 50% increase of the Base Sum Insured for each claim-free year.
- **Claims Shield**: Pays for usually non-payable consumables (gloves, masks, cotton, bandages).

## 5. Optional Benefits & Customizations
- **Aggregate Deductible**: Discount offered if insured opts to bear a deductible amount on all admissible claims.
- **Co-payment**: Insured can opt for a 10% or 20% co-payment on each claim (after deductible).
- **Enhanced Cumulative Bonus Safeguard**: Protects the ECB percentage if the claim amount in a year is ₹1 Lac or less (Not applicable to Platinum Infinite plan).
- **Wellness Benefit**: AI Personal Fitness Coaching, Dietician/Nutrition e-consultation, Unlimited Gym Membership.
- **Walk Healthy Benefit**: Renewal premium discount based on mobile app health points.
- **Additional Basic Sum Insured for Accident**: Doubles the Base Sum Insured for In-Patient treatment required strictly due to accidental injury.

## 6. Underwriting Exclusions & Waiting Periods
**Standard Exclusions Include**:
- Investigation and evaluation purposes only.
- Obesity / Weight control (unless qualifying for Bariatric cover).
- Cosmetic / Plastic surgery.
- Hazardous / Adventure sports.
- Breach of Law.
- Alcoholism, drug or substance abuse.
- Unproven treatments.
- Sterility and infertility.

*Note: Declared Pre-Existing Diseases (PED), such as Hypertension, are covered after the standard Waiting Period is completed without break in renewals (e.g., 2 years as per Mr. Puri's illustration).*
