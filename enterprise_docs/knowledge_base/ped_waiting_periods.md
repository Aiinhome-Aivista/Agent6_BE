# Policy Wording: Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Periods

**Document ID**: UW-MAN-002
**Applies To**: All Health Retail Products

## 1. General PED Waiting Period Clause
Any condition, ailment, or injury for which the insured person had signs or symptoms, and/or was diagnosed, and/or received medical advice/treatment within 48 months prior to the first policy issued by the Insurer, will be classified as a Pre-Existing Disease (PED). 

No claims relating to PEDs will be admissible until the completion of **48 consecutive months** of continuous coverage.

*Exceptions*: 
- Platinum Plan has a reduced PED waiting period of **24 consecutive months**.
- If the policyholder opts for the "PED Buyback Rider", the waiting period is reduced to 12 months.

## 2. Specific Named Ailment Waiting Periods (24 Months)
Regardless of whether they are pre-existing or not, the following conditions have a strict 24-month waiting period from policy inception:
- Cataract
- Benign Prostatic Hypertrophy
- Myomectomy, Hysterectomy (unless for malignancy)
- All types of Hernia, Hydrocele
- Fissures/Fistula in anus, Hemorrhoids/Piles
- Joint replacement surgeries (unless caused by an accident)

## 3. Initial Waiting Period (30 Days)
Any illness contracted within 30 days of the inception date of the Policy is strictly excluded, except for claims arising out of accidents.
